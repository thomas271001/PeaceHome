Source: `Community_Screening.pdf` (one page, Malayalam). Given by the Peace Home director as the
nephrologist-approved screening form. It replaces the earlier 37-question draft (v0.1) completely.

The Malayalam below is copied from the PDF. The English is a working translation made for this app
and should be confirmed by someone fluent in both languages.

## The pathway
1. **Community screening.** *If any item in the first question applies, or the score of the items in the second
   question is **four or more**, you should have the kidney screening test.*
2. **Kidney screening test**
   - Urine Routine: routine urine examination
   - Albumin / Creatinine ratio
   - Blood Creatinine Test: blood test for creatinine
3. **Consultation with a kidney specialist (nephrologist)**
4. **Follow-up**

## Points in the form that need a decision
1. **Threshold wording differs.** Part II's note says "more than four" (5 or more). Step 1 says "four or more" (4 or more).
   The app uses **4 or more**, the stricter-to-miss, safer reading for a screening, and it is one number in the rules page.
2. **Age 60.** The form says "above 60" for the 2-point band and "40 to 59" for the 1-point band. The app puts exactly 60 in the 2-point band.
3. **Part I items 3 and 4** (kidney disease already confirmed, past dialysis) are treated like every other Part I item:
   they qualify for the screening test. The form does not say they are handled differently.
4. **"Excess weight"** has no BMI or other cut-off on the form, so it is a plain self-report tick.
5. **Nothing is written for people who do not qualify.** The app's wording for them is ours.
